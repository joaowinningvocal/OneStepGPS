What ClubLifter is

A control room for VIP nightlife transport.

Bookings come in from hosts, promoters, and partner venues. ClubLifter assigns the right driver and car, routes the pickup, keeps the guest informed by text, and gives dispatch a live map of every vehicle — so nobody is left waiting on a curb.

🎟️

Book & assign

Guests are scheduled with their package, pickup spot, and destination. The system picks an available driver and car automatically, or dispatch does it by hand.

🛰️

Track live

Every vehicle reports its position in real time. Dispatch sees who's moving, who's idle, and exactly which guest each driver is heading to.

💬

Keep guests moving

Automatic texts fire as the driver approaches — one kilometer out, five hundred meters, and on arrival — so the guest is ready at the curb.

The flow of a pickup

From booking to bottle service.

STEP 01

Request

A host or partner books the guest with their package, pickup point, and venue.

STEP 02

Assign

ClubLifter matches an available driver and car, then routes the pickup.

STEP 03

Approach

The guest gets live text updates as the driver closes in on the pickup spot.

STEP 04

Arrive

Driver confirms arrival, the guest is picked up, and dispatch marks the ride complete.
