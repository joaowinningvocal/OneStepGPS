Delete your account

You can permanently delete your ClubLifter account and personal data at any time.

Option 1 — In the app

The fastest way to delete your account:

  1. Open the ClubLifter app
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete my account"
  4. Confirm — your account and data are permanently removed

Option 2 — Request here

Enter the phone number on your account and we'll permanently delete it.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile (name and phone number)
  • Your loyalty points and level
  • Your check-in and visit history

This action cannot be undone. Deletion is immediate. If you have any trouble, contact us and we'll handle it within 30 days.
